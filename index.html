Key Information

What Every Factory Worker Should Know

Time Matters

At 85 dB, you can safely be exposed for 8 hours. At 95 dB, that drops to just 4 hours. Every 3 dB increase halves safe exposure time.

Permanent Damage

Noise-induced hearing loss is irreversible. Once the tiny hair cells in your inner ear are damaged, they cannot regenerate.

Protection Works

Properly fitted earplugs can reduce noise by 15-30 dB. Earmuffs can reduce noise by 20-30 dB. Combined, even better!

Regular Testing

Annual hearing tests (audiograms) can detect early signs of hearing loss before you notice symptoms yourself.

Factory Floor Guide

Identify High-Risk Zones

Hover over different areas to learn about noise hazards

Office Area

Office Area - LOW RISK

Typical Level: 50-65 dB

Generally safe. Normal conversation possible.

PPE: Not required

Assembly Line

Assembly Line - MODERATE RISK

Typical Level: 75-85 dB

Prolonged exposure may cause hearing damage.

PPE: Earplugs recommended

Welding Station

Welding Station - HIGH RISK

Typical Level: 85-95 dB

Hearing protection mandatory. Limit exposure time.

PPE: Earplugs or earmuffs required

Press Room

Press Room - VERY HIGH RISK

Typical Level: 95-110 dB

Immediate hearing damage possible without protection.

PPE: Double protection required

Grinding Area

Grinding Area - VERY HIGH RISK

Typical Level: 100-115 dB

Extremely hazardous. Strict time limits apply.

PPE: Double protection + rotation

Break Room

Break Room - SAFE ZONE

Typical Level: 45-60 dB

Quiet area for hearing recovery.

PPE: Not required
